About Us

The Evidence Intelligence Project organizes biomedical research, evaluates study quality, and estimates how confident we can be in health claims. Every score is transparent — you can see which studies back a claim and how strong each piece of evidence is.

Methodology

Each study is scored on design quality — study type, randomization, blinding, placebo control, sample size, and statistical reporting. Those per-study scores are combined into a confidence level for the claim itself, so you always see the evidence behind a score, not just the number.
